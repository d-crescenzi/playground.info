Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EspToolbox ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you access or use the EspToolbox website and mobile application, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - Regulation EU 2016/679) and applicable data protection legislation.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller responsible for personal data processed through the Service is EspToolbox. For any inquiries, requests, or to exercise your privacy rights, please reach out through the contact details provided in the "Contact Information" section.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on your usage of the application and site, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Information provided voluntarily: customer support inquiries, feedback, or contact details sent directly by you.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: device model, operating system version, anonymous crash logs, and general performance metrics necessary to diagnose bugs.
  • In-App Purchase verification: purchase status tokens and receipt validation data handled via Google Play Billing. We do not process, collect, or store your credit card or financial payment details.

3. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

We process personal data only for specific, legitimate purposes:

  • Service Delivery: ensuring proper operation of the app, licensing verification, and feature availability (Legal basis: contractual necessity).
  • Support and Communication: responding to user questions and resolving technical issues (Legal basis: legitimate interest or contractual necessity).
  • Security and Stability: troubleshooting crashes, preventing fraud, and maintaining application security (Legal basis: legitimate interest).
  • Legal Compliance: complying with applicable statutory, accounting, or regulatory requirements (Legal basis: legal obligation).

4. Third-Party Services

The Service may integrate with trusted third-party providers for critical functions:

  • Google Play Billing / Google Play Services: used for payment processing, app updates, and license verification. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We retain collected information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable laws. When no longer needed, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR (Articles 15-22), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request rectification of incorrect or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Request restriction of or object to data processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with the updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
